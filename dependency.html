How This Works

Companies with 5+ DoD SBIR awards (2020-2024) are cross-referenced with USAspending.gov to find their other federal contract obligations.

*Important: USAspending.gov tracks federal contracts separately from SBIR/STTR awards (which are classified as grants/assistance). The "Other Fed" column shows non-SBIR federal contracts only. Companies showing 100% dependency have minimal federal contracts beyond their SBIR awards.

% SBIR = SBIR Awards / (SBIR + Other Federal Contracts). High percentages indicate companies whose federal revenue comes primarily from SBIR rather than production contracts.
