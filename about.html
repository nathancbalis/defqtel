About

Understanding the purpose and scope of this project.

Purpose

DefqTel provides AI-powered analysis of Department of Defense SBIR (Small Business Innovation Research) awards. By classifying awards across technology and platform dimensions, it enables stakeholders to identify investment trends, growth areas, and emerging priorities in defense R&D.

What SBIR Data Reveals

SBIR awards represent early-stage R&D investments where the DoD is actively seeking innovative solutions. Award patterns indicate:

Technology Priorities

Which capabilities the DoD is investing in developing.

Platform Focus

Which domains are receiving modernization investment.

Growth Trajectories

Year-over-year changes in funding allocation.

Geographic Distribution

Regional concentrations of defense innovation activity.

Target Audience

Contractors
Identify growth areas for R&D investment, understand competitive landscape, and align capabilities with DoD priorities.
Investors
Validate market demand for defense technology startups and identify emerging investment themes.
Researchers
Track DoD priorities and identify funding opportunities aligned with research capabilities.
Analysts
Access structured data for defense market analysis and technology trend reporting.

Current dataset covers DoD SBIR awards from 2020 through 2024. Data is sourced from SBIR.gov, the official U.S. government repository.

Scope Limitations

- This tracker covers SBIR awards only, not total DoD R&D spending or procurement contracts.
- Classification is automated and may not reflect official DoD categorization schemes.
- Awards for classified programs may have generic titles that affect classification accuracy.
- This is an independent analysis tool, not affiliated with the Department of Defense.
