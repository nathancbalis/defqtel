Questions

What format is the data in?

Each purchase includes both CSV and JSON formats. CSV is compatible with Excel, Google Sheets, and most data tools. JSON is ideal for programmatic use.

How accurate is the AI classification?

Classification is performed by Claude AI (Anthropic) with an estimated accuracy of 90-95%. Each award is tagged with both technology type and platform domain.

Can I use this data commercially?

Yes. The underlying SBIR award data is public. You're purchasing the categorized, structured dataset and may use it for internal analysis, research, or commercial purposes.
