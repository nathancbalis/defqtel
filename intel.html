January 12, 2026 Critical
SBIR Program

SBIR Reauthorization Standoff Continues

The SBIR/STTR programs remain unauthorized as Congress returns from recess. The January 30 government funding deadline represents the next realistic vehicle for reauthorization, but negotiations remain stalled.

Key developments:

  • Senator Markey introduced compromise bill in late December
  • Senator Ernst continues to block any extension without reforms
  • NIH has terminated all SBIR/STTR funding opportunities
  • Space Force officials publicly warning about acquisition delays

What this means for DefqTel data:

Our 2020-2024 dataset represents the complete "healthy" program years. The 2025 data on SBIR.gov shows only 435 awards (vs. ~6,500/year typical) due to reporting lag and pre-expiration slowdown. We will update when/if the program resumes normal operations.

January 10, 2026 Launch
Platform

DefqTel Public Launch

DefqTel is now publicly available. The platform provides AI-powered analysis of 17,265 DoD SBIR/STTR awards from 2020-2024, representing $10.8B in defense R&D funding.

What's included:

  • Dual-dimension classification: 18 technology categories x 8 platform domains
  • Velocity scoring to identify "heating up" technology areas
  • Geographic analysis by state and city
  • Full awards browser with search and filtering
  • Data export in multiple formats

About This Data

Current Coverage

DefqTel tracks DoD SBIR/STTR awards from 2020-2024, totaling 17,265 awards and $10.8B in funding. This represents the complete dataset during normal program operations.

Update Frequency

SBIR.gov data typically updates quarterly. Due to the current program lapse, new award data will be limited until Congress reauthorizes SBIR/STTR.
